rGV2 - Ghost Valley 2 (SNES)

useful shortcuts

gotta add videos

first pole cut (nisc): mt trick off the ledge into the pole with good alignment and you'll autotrick off it and cut off the first turn. requires that you chain it into the gap cut or you'll smack into the fence/fall off. may skip getting one or two items from the first item set.

the gap cut (nisc): charge jump into the farther tree on the left and you'll autotrick off the tree and make it across the gap. 100% consistent if you're not boosted charge jumping, can seemingly randomly fail otherwise. obviously, the fence has to be gone or you'll just smack it.

the gap cut (shroom): shroom towards the closer tree on the right, trick off the ledge, and you'll autotrick off off the tree make it across the gap.

corner cut (nisc): mt trick off the ledge into the right side of the tree, left side of the tree, or either one of the two poles to the left of the tree and you'll autotrick off it and cut the corner. using the right side of the tree is fastest since it cuts the corner the most.

corner cut (feather): trick off the ledge into the pole to the right of the tree and you'll autotrick off it, then feather to reach the road at/past the second item set.

goop cut (nisc): trick into the pole before the track dips into the poison and you'll jump over it.

item spots

using the bullet bill in/after the gap cut takes you to the final turn, just make sure you use it a good way through the gap, or else it'll go around which is pretty slow.

you can also use the bill just after the second item set and it's pretty fast there (you can even jump off the right side of the track and it'll cut that corner), taking you all the way to the finish line, but make sure that you do it AFTER YOU CUT THE CORNER, or else you may get a really bad slowdown.

using the bill anywhere else could have you skip the gap cut if you don't pass enough people, which is pretty bad, so try to avoid that.

when you pull a golden mushroom from the first item set, even if you use it immediately and don't spam, it will last until the second set, so it may be hard to chain for 2 items if you don't fall off.
